# 0001. Record architecture decisions

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-03

## Context
Ouril spans web, mobile and (later) a backend, and it will grow over several phases. We need to remember *why* we made choices, not just what we chose.

## Decision
Record significant decisions as short Architecture Decision Records in `docs/decisions/`, following the template in the [index](README.md).

## Alternatives considered
- **No written record:** the reasoning gets lost, and decisions get reopened again and again.
- **A wiki outside the repo:** it drifts away from the code.

## Consequences
Every major choice needs a short write-up. In return, newcomers (and our future selves) can see what we decided and why.
