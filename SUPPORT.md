# Getting help

> Where to ask questions and report problems. **Status:** Draft

- **How do I play?** Read the [rules](docs/game/rules.md), with worked examples.
- **Questions and ideas:** start a [discussion](https://github.com/Alfredo-Moreira/Ouril/discussions).
- **Something is broken:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/Alfredo-Moreira/Ouril/issues/new/choose).
- **"That's not how we play it here":** open a **rule or variant report** from the [same page](https://github.com/Alfredo-Moreira/Ouril/issues/new/choose). Reports from players are how we get the rules right for every country.
- **Security vulnerability:** report it privately, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Contributing:** see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [docs](docs/README.md).

Ouril is a small volunteer project, so replies may take a few days.
